Day-by-Day Plan

A framework to confirm with Captain Zack — actual anchorages and pacing are normally worked out with him once aboard, based on weather, crowds, and what the group wants to do.

Per Vagabonde's official listing, Mah Na Mah Na's home base is Yacht Haven Grande (YHG), St. Thomas, but Red Hook is the confirmed preferred pick-up/drop-off location for guests (American Yacht Harbor is also available as an alternate). The plan below uses Red Hook throughout.
Day 1 — Thu, Jan 21, 2027 Arrival & Boarding
Fly into St. Thomas, board Mah Na Mah Na
  • Land at STT 1:05pm (JetBlue B6 807, nonstop from BOS)
  • Arrange ground transportation from the airport to Red Hook, St. Thomas — Vagabonde's confirmed preferred pick-up/drop-off location; specific transport method TBD (taxi, private car, or Vagabonde-arranged pickup)
  • Check into Mah Na Mah Na at Red Hook
  • Provision for about an hour (groceries, ice, last-minute supplies)
  • Set sail for St. John for the first overnight
Night 1 — Thu, Jan 21 Overnight
Overnight at St. John, USVI
  • Anchor or moor off St. John for the night (specific bay TBD with Captain Zack — Cruz Bay and Coral Bay are the usual options)
  • First evening aboard — settle in, welcome dinner
Day 2 — Fri, Jan 22, 2027 BVI Check-In
Sail to West End, Tortola — check into the BVI
  • Depart St. John and sail across to West End (Soper's Hole), Tortola
  • Clear BVI Customs & Immigration at West End — passports required for all 6 guests
  • Once cleared, free to explore BVI waters for the rest of the charter
Days 3–6 — Sat, Jan 23 – Tue, Jan 26, 2027 BVI Cruising
Island-hop the BVI
Route and pacing to be worked out day-to-day with Captain Zack once aboard. Spots Vagabonde has mentioned working into USVI/BVI routes:
  • Soggy Dollar Bar (Jost Van Dyke)
  • The Willy T (Norman Island)
  • North Sound area — Bitter End or Saba Rock (Virgin Gorda)
  • Foxy's (Jost Van Dyke)
These are suggestions from Vagabonde's earlier notes, not a fixed itinerary — the exact order and additional stops (e.g. The Baths, Cane Garden Bay) would be confirmed with Captain Zack based on weather and how crowded each anchorage is.
Day 7 — Wed, Jan 27, 2027 Return Crossing
Check out of the BVI, sail back to St. Thomas
  • Clear BVI Customs on the way out (likely West End again, though exact checkpoint TBD)
  • Sail back to St. Thomas and spend the last night moored/anchored there — no rush the next morning, since the return flight isn't until 2:00pm

What's Included & What's Not

Mah Na Mah Na is a captain-only charter, so the $17,000 rate bundles a lot in — it doesn't break out into separate line items the way a bareboat quote does.

Included in $17,000
  • Yacht + captain fee
  • Fuel
  • Ice
  • Mooring & dock fees
  • Customs fees
  • Ship's bar
  • Breakfast items
Not included
  • Main meals/provisions beyond the light breakfast — budget roughly $200–$250 per person for the week
  • Feeding the captain if the group dines out (invite along, or give cash for their own meal)
  • Crew gratuity — 10–20% of the yacht price, roughly $1,700–$3,400 for the week
  • Airport transfers and any self-booked shore excursions
  • Travel insurance — purchased separately, per couple, through Rick Bagnall at Risk Strategies
Provisioning happens on Day 1 at Red Hook (about an hour, per the plan above) — worth deciding ahead of time how much cooking-aboard vs. eating-ashore the group wants, since that swings the $200–$250pp estimate quite a bit.
Travel insurance: Vagabonde recommends purchasing adequate travel insurance and works with Rick Bagnall at Brown & Brown (Risk Strategies), who specializes in yacht charter insurance — p: 860-391-7161, Rick.Bagnall@bbrown.com. A "Cancel For Any Reason" (CFAR) policy generally needs to be purchased within 14–21 days of the first payment/deposit. The group can use its own insurance instead, as long as it's adequate for the trip. Travel Insurance Questionnaire (PDF)
Deposit schedule: 50% deposit paid. Remaining 50% due in December.
Contract: Final, fully executed Charter Yacht Brokers Association (CYBA) contract — the industry standard across the Caribbean, covering payment terms, cancellation policies, and yacht conditions. All parties have signed. Signed CYBA Contract (PDF)
Payment instructions: Full wire/payment details are on pages 2 & 3 of the invoice Jake sends — any questions on process, wire details, or timing go straight to him. Invoice AD701 (PDF)
Archive: Payment methods & signing process Historical — the charter is now confirmed and signed
Payment methods: Credit card is accepted but carries a 3.8% processing fee, so Jake at Above Deck much prefers ACH/wire — most clients pay that way. It's fine to split it, e.g. some couples pay by ACH/wire and others by credit card. For gratuity specifically, cash or Venmo is absolutely fine (also accepted: PayPal, Zelle, Apple Pay, per Vagabonde's listing).
Signing process: Once the draft is reviewed and everyone's ready to move forward, Jake sends a separate email from "ZohoSign" for signing online — check junk/spam if it doesn't show up, since these automated emails sometimes get filtered. After the group signs, Jake sends it to the yacht owner for their signature. The charter is officially confirmed once (1) all parties have signed and (2) the deposit has been received in Above Deck's client escrow account.
Contract & payment contact: Jake at Above Deck handles contracts and funds for Vagabonde's Virgin Islands partner boats. He's a former captain who worked primarily in the Virgin Islands and Croatia.
