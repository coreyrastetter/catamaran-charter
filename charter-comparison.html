FAQ Summary

Answers pulled directly from the charter companies' emails. Anything they didn't clearly answer is marked UNKNOWN rather than guessed.

Is the Yacht Damage Waiver only for bareboat charters?
No — The Moorings offers the Yacht Damage Waiver (YDW) on all boats they offer. It's optional but recommended as the boat-insurance option (an alternative is paying a higher security deposit at the base instead).Source: Arelis Marin, The Moorings, 29-Jul-26
What does the Travel Protection Plan cost, and is it the same as the crewed-charter travel insurance?
The Travel Protection Plan (TPP) costs 8% of the total trip cost; a Cancel For Any Reason (CFAR) upgrade costs 12.8% of total trip cost. Whether this is the same product as the "optional travel insurance" listed on the crewed-charter page is UNKNOWN — that specific follow-up wasn't directly confirmed in the emails reviewed.Source: Arelis Marin, The Moorings, 29-Jul-26
Is there an early-booking discount on the skippered bareboat quote?
No. The Moorings' stated policy is that they don't currently offer early-booking discounts for crewed charters, and in practice, quote TA0576 (skippered bareboat) shows no early-booking line — while the bareboat (TA0580) and crewed (TA0581) quotes both do show one (–$1,049.90 and –$1,699.90 respectively). The Grenada and St. Lucia skippered quotes (SZ5932, SZ5984) also show no early-booking line.Source: Arelis Marin, The Moorings, 29-Jul-26; quotes TA0576/TA0580/TA0581/SZ5932/SZ5984
Are other discounts available (GLOBE, AAA, AARP, etc.)?
None available at the moment, per Arelis — though she offered to check with her supervisor for any additional help.Source: Arelis Marin, The Moorings, 29-Jul-26
Why is Wi-Fi only shown as included on one quote?
It comes down to yacht class, not charter style: the skippered bareboat quote (TA0576) is a "Classic Class" yacht (3+ years old), where Wi-Fi is an add-on and appears as its own $185 line item. The bareboat quote (TA0580) is on an "Exclusive Class" yacht (1–3 years old), which includes Wi-Fi at no extra charge — so there's no separate line for it. All crewed charters include Wi-Fi.Source: Arelis Marin, The Moorings, 29-Jul-26
Can we add sleep-aboard or a hotel for the night of 4/24, since the flight lands after the last ferry?
Sleep-aboard is reflected on the bareboat/skippered quotes (see the "Sleep Aboard" lines on TA0576, SZ5932, and SZ5984). It is not available for crewed charters — for the crewed option, a hotel can be arranged instead through The Moorings' Pre-Travel team once the charter is confirmed.Source: Arelis Marin, The Moorings, 29-Jul-26; quotes TA0576, SZ5932, SZ5984
Our flight lands in St. Thomas at 3pm — how late can we arrive to the boat?
Base staff typically leave around 9:00 PM, but there's always someone available to welcome guests arriving later.Source: Arelis Marin, The Moorings, 29-Jul-26
How do we get from St. Thomas to the boat in Tortola — ferry or water taxi?
You can arrange the ferry yourself, or use a private water taxi. Chillout Charters quoted a private water taxi between Cruz Bay/Red Hook and The Moorings' Road Town base at $180/person one-way (all fees included) for a group of 6, or $2,160 round trip ($720/couple) — plus a $350/each-way surcharge if you'd rather be picked up/dropped at Charlotte Amalie's Crown Bay Marina near the airport (weather dependent). Separate ground transport from the St. Thomas airport to Red Hook is paid in cash to the driver: $135 fixed for 1–5 passengers, or $25/person for 6 or more.Source: Arelis Marin, The Moorings, 29-Jul-26; Suzanne Fonseca, Chillout Charters, 28-Jul-26
The charter ends at 11am — is that enough time to make a 1:45pm or 3:40pm flight out of St. Thomas?
A 1:45 PM flight would be very tight, especially given the transfer time from Tortola. The Moorings recommends the 3:40 PM flight instead. Flying out of Tortola (Beef Island) directly is easier and faster than routing through St. Thomas.Source: Arelis Marin, The Moorings, 29-Jul-26
What exactly is included with the skippered charter?
The skipper and their own provisions are included. The group's own food and beverages are separate and arranged at your own expense.Source: Arelis Marin, The Moorings, 29-Jul-26
Is there a credit card surcharge for a Massachusetts billing address?
No — The Moorings confirmed there's no credit card surcharge for Massachusetts billing addresses (the surcharge policy also exempts Connecticut and Puerto Rico billing addresses, per the quotes' fine print).Source: Arelis Marin, The Moorings, 29-Jul-26; quote terms
What's the payment schedule — deposit, balance, and how are incidentals handled?
Initial payment is 50%, with the remaining balance due in December. The Travel Protection Plan must be paid in full at time of purchase, so the first payment may run slightly higher than the second. Payments can be split across multiple credit cards. How security-deposit incidentals are specifically billed at the end of the charter is UNKNOWN beyond the general Yacht Damage Waiver option.Source: Arelis Marin, The Moorings, 29-Jul-26
How much food and drink is included on the fully crewed charter — is extra provisioning available?
The Moorings doesn't have an exact amount to share — provisioning for crewed charters is typically coordinated with the Pre-Travel team and the onboard chef based on the group's preferences and menu. They said they rarely hear of guests needing additional provisioning, but were following up to confirm.Source: Arelis Marin, The Moorings, 29-Jul-26
What's not included on the Abaco partner-boat (skippered) quotes?
Food, fuel, and mooring fees are excluded from all three Abaco partner-boat quotes. An optional chef is available for $2,550 for the week — Vagabonde recommends booking the full week rather than a night or two, since it's more of an all-or-nothing value proposition.Source: Jack Karger, Vagabonde Adventures, 29-Jul-26
Has Corey & Tina's group shared a budget with Vagabonde Adventures yet?
UNKNOWN — Vagabonde asked for a per-couple or total budget before preparing a full Abaco proposal; there's no reply in the thread yet confirming one was shared.Source: Jack Karger, Vagabonde Adventures, 29-Jul-26
Is full-crew available in Grenada or St. Lucia?
No — The Moorings confirmed they don't currently have availability for a fully crewed charter in either Grenada or St. Lucia for the requested dates. Both destinations were quoted as bareboat-with-skipper instead.Source: Arelis Marin, The Moorings, 24-Jul-26; quotes SZ5932, SZ5984
Why are the Grenada/St. Lucia quotes on different dates than the BVI/Abaco quotes?
Grenada (SZ5932) and St. Lucia (SZ5984) were both quoted for Jan 29 – Feb 4, 2027 — a late-January window rather than the April 24–May 1 window used for the BVI and Abaco quotes. UNKNOWN whether Moorings can re-quote Grenada/St. Lucia for the April dates as well.Source: quotes SZ5932, SZ5984
